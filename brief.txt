IzuTech (izutech.fr) — site brief, October 2026

Practical AI automation for French SMBs, run by one engineer based in Pau. French first, English available.

Language: the page is written in French. script.js holds the English text. English shows when the visitor picks it (EN button, remembered on that browser), when the link has ?lang=en, or when the device language is not French.

Offers (starting prices, fixed quote after the audit):
- Automated data processing: from €900
- Internal assistant for your documents: from €1,200
- Customer support co-pilot: from €1,800
- Workshop "build your own agent with Claude": from €450 (half day, up to 6 people, 1-hour follow-up)

Method: free 30-min call → audit → prototype in 1–2 weeks → rollout in 4–8 weeks → 30 days of support.
Booking: Google Calendar booking page https://calendar.app.google/Y9YtHrf6hDHfdrVC7 · Contact form: Formspree · Email: contact@izutech.fr

Demos (demo.html + demo.js): a site assistant that answers only from the site's content and cites sources (mini version of the €1,200 offer), an invoice-reading demo with automatic checks (€900 offer), and a time-saved calculator (runs in the browser). Video slots are in the page, hidden until the videos exist. The two AI demos call a Cloudflare Worker (worker/) that holds the Mistral API key; its address goes in <body data-api=""> on demo.html. The assistant's knowledge lives in worker/src/knowledge.js: update it when the site text changes.

Files: index.html, demo.html, styles.css, script.js (language switch, all pages), demo.js, worker/, photo/. technical.html only redirects old links to the home page.
Voice: warm, direct, no jargon, no unproven numbers.
